# Cyprus dataset attribution

Lefteris Alambritis, Joep Immanuel Blanksma, Lenos Hadjidemetriou,
Sotiris Shiacallis, Stelios Philippou and Angellos Christofi (2026),
“Wind Generation, Photovoltaic Generation and Meteorological Datasets
with 30 seconds Resolution from an Actual Site in Cyprus”. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20800202

The dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The quality-check CSV is derived from the May 2025 file: it identifies
complete 30-second grids and calculates hourly sampled mean PV power only for
complete hours. Missing or rejected measurements are not filled with zero.

The evaluation also uses Open-Meteo Previous Runs weather data; downloaded
weather files are not included in this commit. Review applicable Open-Meteo
terms before commercial use. The results are exploratory, with provisional
meter/weather alignment, not operational validation or cross-site transfer.
